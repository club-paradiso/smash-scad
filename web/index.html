Crit week got you? Go ahead, wreck the Savannah campus. Every SCAD building here is drawn from its real OpenStreetMap footprint.

  • Tap a building to drop the selected weapon
  • Phone: one finger moves, two fingers rotate and zoom
  • Desktop: drag to move, right-drag or Shift-drag to rotate, wheel to zoom
  • SCAD buildings (yellow) are worth 5× the points

Unofficial fan game, not affiliated with or endorsed by Savannah College of Art and Design. No logos used. Inspired by SMASH 대학교. Building outlines, levels, roof shapes, building parts and courtyards come from OpenStreetMap 3D tags (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Where a building has no height or roof tag, it gets a flat roof and an estimated height.
