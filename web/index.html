Crit week got you? Go ahead, wreck the Savannah campus. Every SCAD building here is drawn from its real OpenStreetMap footprint.

  • Tap a building to drop the selected weapon
  • Phone: one finger moves, two fingers rotate and zoom. The target button shows where you are (your location stays on your phone)
  • Desktop: drag to move, right-drag or Shift-drag to rotate, wheel to zoom
  • SCAD buildings (yellow roofs and name tags) are worth 5× the points

Unofficial fan game, not affiliated with or endorsed by Savannah College of Art and Design. No logos used. Inspired by SMASH 대학교. Building outlines, levels, roof shapes, building parts and courtyards come from OpenStreetMap 3D tags (© OpenStreetMap contributors). SCAD buildings are shaped from USGS 3DEP lidar (2018): roof surface and wall heights as measured, walls along the OSM outline. Wall colours are not measured. Where any other building has no height tag, its height is measured from USGS 3DEP lidar (2018 flight, public domain); if the lidar can't tell (newer building, tree cover), the height is estimated. Buildings without a roof tag get a flat roof. Trees, paths, parking, pools and piers are drawn only where OpenStreetMap maps them.
